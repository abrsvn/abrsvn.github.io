Winter 2014: Meeting 8

Tue 11 March 2014 by Nate Arnett, Adrian Brasoveanu

The last meeting of the quarter will take place this Friday (March 14) in Stevenson 232 (Eye-tracking lab; please note the change of venue) and it will be LASC related. Nate and Adrian will talk about some of their recent language processing research and how it links with issues of grammatical representation. Nate will talk about short-term memory in sentence comprehension and Adrian about inverse scope.
